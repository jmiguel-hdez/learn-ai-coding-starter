# Sloperator Slogans

1. **Sloperator: vibe, nah, ralph — your ideas deserve a clanker.** — Names the three control-flow words; "clanker" sells that anyone can define a function.
2. **Ship it, or hallucinate about it. Sloperator.** — Everything a program does ends in `ship` or `hallucinate`; also winks at the confident nonsense of LLM output.
3. **No semicolons. Just sparkles.** ✨ — The whole pitch in seven words: `✨` replaces `;`, and it is far nicer to type.
4. **Write the slop. Keep the loadbearing.** — Plays `slop` against `loadbearing` to say the mutable stuff is cheap and the important bindings stay put.
5. **SlopLang: a language where `❌` is honest and `nah` is final.** — Leans on `❌` and `nah` for a deadpan, skeptical voice that fits the name.
6. **No truthiness. No mercy.** — Conditions must be `✅` or `❌`, so the language never guesses what you meant; blunt and confident, like the spec.
7. **`loadbearing` walls, `slop` floors.** — Splits the two declarations into architecture: the immutable stuff holds the building up, the mutable stuff is where you walk around.
8. **Ralph until it's `❌`. Then stop.** — A plain-English reading of `ralph`, the only loop, and a wink at how little control flow the language actually has.
9. **Every program ends in `ship` or `hallucinate`.** — States the two exits from any function, and doubles as a motto for shipping code instead of just talking about it.
10. **500 clankers deep, then the stack says nah.** — Turns the call-depth cap into a punchline: even the recursion limit speaks SlopLang.
